Clan History

The Ngassa Clan originated in the Littoral region of Cameroon around 1902, founded by Pierre Ngassa who led a migration from the forest regions toward the coast. Over the following decades, clan members spread throughout Cameroon, establishing major branches in Douala, Yaoundé, and Kribi.

The Bulu Tribe, to which the Ngassa Clan belongs, is known for its rich oral traditions and close-knit family structures. Marriage customs have historically required verification of lineage to prevent close-relative unions.
