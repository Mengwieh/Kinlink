Platform overview

How KinLink works

A graph-database approach to genealogy — storing every person as a node, every family bond as an edge, and calculating the shortest kinship path between any two individuals in milliseconds.

01

Create your profile

Register with your full name, clan, village, and of birth. Your profile becomes a node in KinLink's ancestry graph. Add your parents, grandparents, and siblings to begin building your lineage.

User node Clan metadata Village indexing
02

Connect your family tree

Link yourself under your parents and grandparents. Each connection becomes a directed edge in the graph (PARENT_OF / CHILD_OF). Family Heads can verify and approve connections, ensuring accuracy and preventing fake entries.

Graph edges Verified by elders Consent-based
03

Search any person

Use smart fuzzy search to find any person by name, clan, village, or parent name. KinLink matches across the full community graph — not just your own family — using phonetic and token-based matching to handle name variations.

Fuzzy matching Multi-field search Cross-community

04

Instant relationship detection

Select any two people and KinLink runs a bidirectional BFS from both nodes simultaneously, finding the shortest kinship path. The algorithm then classifies the result: cousin degree, shared ancestor, maternal/paternal lineage, degree of separation, and clan connection.

BFS traversal Cousin classification Ancestor tracing
05

Visual path comparison

The relationship result is displayed as an interactive visual path — showing exactly which ancestors connect two people, how many generations back, and through which lineage (maternal or paternal). You can share or screenshot this result.

Path visualization Shareable

Core algorithms

The relationship engine uses proven graph theory — efficient even at millions of nodes.

Graph traversal

Bidirectional BFS

Searches from both persons simultaneously, meeting in the middle at a common ancestor. Reduces time complexity from O(b^d) to O(b^(d/2)) on large graphs.

Kinship math

Cousin degree formula

Degree = min(generations from person A to common ancestor, generations from person B to common ancestor) − 1. "Removed" = absolute difference between the two generation counts.

Lineage

Maternal / paternal tagging

Each PARENT_OF edge is tagged with side=maternal or side=paternal. The path traversal accumulates side tags, so the result can state "related through maternal lineage."

Clustering

Clan grouping

All nodes with a shared clan_id are grouped into a family subgraph. Clan-level relationships are detected even when no direct ancestor path exists in the database.

Data quality

Duplicate detection

AI-assisted deduplication uses name similarity, DOB proximity, village, and shared relative matches to flag potential duplicate profiles before merging.

Database

Neo4j + PostgreSQL

Neo4j handles graph traversal. PostgreSQL stores user accounts, clans, and community metadata. Indexes on name, clan_id, and village enable sub-millisecond search.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before starting your lineage journey.

Can I switch plans at any time?
+
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately and you'll be charged the prorated difference in FCFA. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period.
Is there a free trial?
+
The Mémoire plan is completely free to start — no credit card required. Lignée and Héritage both include a 14-day free trial so you can explore all features before your first payment.
What payment methods are accepted?
+
We accept Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Orange Money), as well as Visa and Mastercard. All transactions are processed securely and displayed in FCFA. No hidden conversion fees.
What is the Family Head role on Héritage?
+
Family Heads are trusted elders or clan administrators who can verify and approve family connections, manage the entire clan's members across all branches, and ensure the integrity of lineage records. This role displays a special elder badge on your profile and is exclusive to the Héritage plan.
Is my family data private and secure?
+
Absolutely. All family data is encrypted at rest and in transit. You control the visibility of your profile and family tree — public, clan-only, family-only, or private. We never sell your data. Sensitive records like dates and locations can be hidden per your privacy settings.
Can I use KinLink for multiple family branches?
+
Yes. Héritage supports unlimited members across all branches. Lignée's 300-member limit covers most extended families. If your needs exceed any plan, contact us for a custom organisation quote.
Do you offer discounts for NGOs or community organisations?
+
Yes — we offer special pricing for verified NGOs, cultural preservation organisations, and community associations in Cameroon and across the CEMAC zone. Contact us with proof of status and we'll set up a custom plan.
